💡 Tips

  • Make sure the QR code is clear and fills a good portion of the image.
  • If your phone camera produces HEIC images, convert them to JPEG first.
  • If decoding fails, try another image or crop to the QR area.
  • Camera scanning works best with good lighting and a steady hand.
