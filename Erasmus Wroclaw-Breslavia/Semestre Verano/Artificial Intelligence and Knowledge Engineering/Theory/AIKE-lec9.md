Maciej Piasecki, G4.19, DAI, WUST  Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering  Overfitting  Neural networks due to large number of parameters are prone to  overfitting, the model fits to the training data set but does not  perform well on the test set.  The most obvious ways to deal with overfitting problem is to:  Increase training data:   Gathering more diverse and  representative data can help the model learn better patterns  and reduce overfitting. If it's feasible, collecting additional data  can be an effective solution.  Reduce model complexity:   Overly complex models, such as  those with a large number of parameters or deep neural  networks, are more prone to overfitting.  Data augmentation:   Augmenting the training data by applying  transformations, for example in case of image data: rotations,  translations, or flips can help increase its diversity.  Noise   –   random distortion added to the training data.

Maciej Piasecki, G4.19, DAI, WUST  Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering  Regularization  Neural architecture and parameters   –   build neural architecture  for given problem and data domain. For example, use CNN for  image classification. When training on new data  reuse already pretrained network,  and fine - tune only few last layers.  Early stopping   –   monitoring the error on the Dev Set and  stopping the training procedure when the error begins to rise.  Trade off between breadth and depth   –   neural network with  more layers tend to require far fewer neurons per layer because  of the composition functions created by successive layers. This  makes possible for network to have less parameters overall  at least two layers for modelling functions of any shape  Dropout/Dropconnect   –   randomly selected units/connections are  not used in the training iteration and excluded from error  propagation. This leads to more expressive and distinct features

Maciej Piasecki, G4.19, DAI, WUST  Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering  Vanishing   and   exploding   gradient  Exploding Gradient   -   when the gradients during  backpropagation become extremely large, it is known as the  exploding gradient problem. As the gradients are  backpropagated through the network, they can multiply  together, resulting in exponentially increasing values. This can  cause the weights to update dramatically, leading to unstable  training and making it difficult for the model to converge. In  extreme cases, the weights may become   NaN   (not a number) or  infinity.  Vanishing Gradient   –   on the other hand, the vanishing gradient  problem occurs when the gradients during backpropagation  become extremely small. As the gradients are backpropagated  through the network, they can diminish exponentially,  particularly in deep networks with many layers. When the  gradients become too small, the weight updates are minimal,  and the model may fail to learn complex patterns effectively.  The early layers of the network are most affected by the  vanishing gradient problem.

Maciej Piasecki, G4.19, DAI, WUST  Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering  Vanishing   and   exploding   gradient   –  solutions   (1)  Gradient clipping:   This technique involves scaling down the  gradients if their norm exceeds a certain threshold. It helps to  prevent the exploding gradient problem by limiting the  magnitude of the gradients.  Batch normalization:   normalizes the activations within each mini -  batch during training. It helps to mitigate the exploding and  vanishing gradient problems by maintaining a more stable  distribution of activations and gradients.  Initialization strategies:   Proper initialization of the weights can  alleviate the vanishing gradient problem. Techniques like Xavier  or   Kaiming   He initialization set the initial weights in a way that  balances the variance of activations and gradients throughout  the network.

Maciej Piasecki, G4.19, DAI, WUST  Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering  Vanishing   and   exploding   gradient   –  solutions   (2)  Non - saturating activation functions:   ReLU   (Rectified Linear Unit)  and its variants are less prone to the vanishing gradient problem  compared to sigmoid or tanh functions. They allow gradients to  flow more easily through the network.  Neural network modifications:   using residual connections, which  allow gradients to propagate more easily or special types of  recurrent networks: LSTM (Long Short - Term Memory) and GRU  (Gated Recurrent Unit).

Maciej Piasecki, G4.19, DAI, WUST  Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering  Epoch,   batch   and   minibatch  Epoch   is a complete pass through the entire training dataset.  Batch   gradient descent is an optimizing step over all training  instances. This leads to global optimum, but it is not used in  practice as it is very slow to compute. That is why in practice  batch size   is a size of a   minibatch .  gradients = average gradients across the batch samples  batch size: s=1 (gradient descent), s=m (mini - batch), s=n (batch, full batch)  Minibatch   is related to   stochastic gradient descent   ( SGD ), where  a small number of training examples is taken at each step to  perform a network update. This leads to more frequent updates,  but it can’t be guaranteed they lead towards global optimum.
