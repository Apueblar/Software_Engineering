Maciej Piasecki, G4.19, DAI, WUST  Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Engineering   9  Deduction   vs   Induction  Deduction   – inference from general to specific (fact) – top-  down:  Known:   All dogs have four legs.  We know that:   Patcher is my dog.  We deduct:   Patcher has four legs.  Induction – inference from specific (facts) to general – bottom-  up:  We see:  one leaf is green and thin,  next leaf is green, thin and oval,  next – green, thin, and oblong, …  We induct:  All leaves are green and thin.
