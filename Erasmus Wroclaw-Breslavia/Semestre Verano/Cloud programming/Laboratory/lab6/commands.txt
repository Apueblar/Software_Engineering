By running the commands:
> scp -i labsuser.pem labsuser.pem ec2-user@52.204.28.165:~
Where 52.204.28.165 is the Public IP of Public VM1

We connect by:
> ssh -i labsuser.pem ec2-user@52.204.28.165

Enable permission:
> chmod 400 labsuser.pem

And by running:
> ssh -i labsuser.pem ec2-user@10.0.2.153
Where 10.0.2.153 is the Private IP of Private VM2
